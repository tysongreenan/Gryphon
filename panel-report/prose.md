# Prose Critic — homepage copy

**Phase:** 4 · **Scope:** marketing strings only (not demo UI microcopy unless it sells)

---

## Voice (what works)

- Short, concrete, product-true: “Agents that never die on a login.”
- Scope disclaimers are sharp: “Not an agent framework · not a browser platform…”
- Avoids generic “supercharge your AI” sludge in the hero

---

## Line-level suggestions

| ID | Location | Current | Suggestion |
|----|----------|---------|------------|
| **R1** | System H2 | “Three things, and *nothing else.*” (two fonts) | Same words, **one font**; or “Three things. Nothing else.” |
| **R2** | System 01 body | Long warm-session anecdote | Cut to 2 sentences: persist context · no cookie plumbing |
